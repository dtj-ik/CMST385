ABOUT

Manolo Cabrera and Hugo Lara make up ROZ, an up-and-coming DJ and producer duo in the Mexican music scene. As of today, they have over 30 million streams on streaming platforms and over 1.4 million monthly listeners. They promise to be one of the electronic music projects in Mexico that will define a new sound for the genre, seeking not only to make a musical impact but also to gain a presence at festivals, events, and clubs. They have managed to collaborate with leading figures in the Latin American industry such as Tom & Collins, Maye (songwriter for Danny Ocean, The Marias), Yng Lvcas, and others.

Recently, they sold out two shows at Supremo and Foro Indie Rocks in Mexico City, both with over 1,000 attendees. They have managed to perform and make appearances at world-class events like Formula 1 at the Mexican Grand Prix and festivals such as Tecate Emblema. Additionally, they have been invited by international artists like Deadmau5 and John Summit to open their shows.
